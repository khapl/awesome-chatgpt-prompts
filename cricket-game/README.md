# 🏏 Cricket Batting Challenge

An interactive web-based cricket batting game where you face 20 overs and try to score as many runs as possible!

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Click "Start Match" to begin
3. Choose your shot for each delivery:
   - **Defend** (🛡️): Safe shot, no runs but minimal risk
   - **Single** (🏃): Low risk, score 1-2 runs
   - **Boundary** (⚡): Medium risk, score 4 runs
   - **Six** (🚀): High risk, score 6 runs

## 🎯 Objective

- Score **150 runs** or more to win!
- You have **120 balls** (20 overs) to achieve the target
- Don't lose all **10 wickets**!

## 📊 Features

- **Real-time Scoreboard**: Track your score, wickets, balls faced, and run rate
- **Ball-by-Ball Commentary**: Dynamic commentary for each delivery
- **Risk vs Reward**: Choose between safe and aggressive shots
- **Visual Animations**: See the ball being bowled and hit
- **Current Over Display**: Track the last 6 balls of your innings
- **Detailed Statistics**: View comprehensive stats at the end of the match
- **High Score Tracking**: Beat your personal best!

## 🎲 Game Mechanics

### Shot Types & Risks

| Shot | Runs | Wicket Risk | Success Rate |
|------|------|-------------|--------------|
| Defend | 0 | 2% | 98% |
| Single | 1-2 | 10% | 90% |
| Boundary | 4 | 25% | 60% |
| Six | 6 | 40% | 40% |

### Ball Types

The bowler delivers different types of balls, each with varying difficulty:
- Good Length
- Full Toss (easier to hit)
- Bouncer
- Yorker (harder to play)
- Spin
- Slower Ball

## 🏆 Winning Conditions

- Score 150+ runs before losing 10 wickets
- Score 150+ runs within 120 balls

## 💡 Strategy Tips

1. **Start Safely**: Use defend and singles early to get settled
2. **Build Momentum**: Look for boundaries once you're comfortable
3. **Calculate Risk**: Watch the scoreboard and plan your approach
4. **Watch the Run Rate**: Keep track of required run rate vs current run rate
5. **Save Wickets**: You need wickets in hand to score big in the final overs
